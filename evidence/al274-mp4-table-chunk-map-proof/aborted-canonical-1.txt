# aborted canonical lane trial 1

The first canonical group run was manually aborted before completion. This was not a product counterexample and not a proof failure. The harness `mp4_stsc_chunk_samples_len2_chunks0_1_bounded_exact`, which sequentially verified chunks=0 and chunks=1 in one harness, reached CBMC RSS 4,891,052 KiB at 00:28 with `--unwind 4`. The two shapes were split into separate harnesses to keep each proof inside the 4 GiB design budget. The group becomes 14 proofs and the registry 56 entries.
